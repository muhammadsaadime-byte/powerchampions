Except as noted, this policy is not applicable to information gathered or provided to us pursuant to other specific agreements.

In order to maximize your experience and provide our Services, we may process personal information from the categories listed below. Our listing of these categories does not mean that all categories or the examples listed in each category are collected from everyone who uses our Services or otherwise interacts with us. We are providing a detailed list here in the spirit of transparency.

- **Identifiers:** This may include your name, address, email address, telephone number, IP address, cookies and other similar online identifiers.
- **Identity document information:** This may include driver’s license numbers, dates of birth, and social security numbers.
- **Internet or other electronic network activity:** This may include your browser type, browser identifier, session information, site navigation data, and other similar information (collected via the use of cookies, and other similar technologies).
- **Geolocation data:** This may include your approximate location inferred from IP address or interaction with geotargeted ads.
- **Commercial information:** This may include purchase invoices, records of services purchased and/or considered, payment information, and business contact information.
- **Audio, electronic, visual, thermal, olfactory, or similar information:** This may include audio data gathered during phone calls and photos/videos uploaded by our customers.
- **Professional or Employment-Related Information:** This may include your business/employer name and business contact information including but not limited to employee ID#, employer responsibility center, and employer address.

#### Sources of Personal Information We Collect

We collect the categories of personal information listed above from various sources. This section lists and describes those sources.

**Directly from you** . We collect personal information directly from you when you provide it to us, for example, when you submit a service request/quote; subscribe to email updates; upload or submit any content or other material through the Services; complete vendor onboarding documents; submit a job application or complete recruitment forms; or request information or contact us with inquiries through the Services or via email.

**Indirectly from you** . We may automatically collect personal information from you when you visit the Services through the use of tracking technologies or by other passive means. This “passively collected” information helps us to improve our offerings and allows us to ensure our Services work as designed and in a way that meets your expectations. Some examples include:

- **Log Information.** Through the use of analytics tools, we may automatically record information that your browser or mobile device sends when you access our Services. This information may include information such as your type, browser language, your computer operating system, your mobile device and mobile operating system, the name of your internet service provider or your mobile carrier, the date and time of your request, etc.

-
**Cookies and Similar Tracking Technologies.** When you visit or access the Services, we may utilize cookies (small text files containing a string of characters sent to your computer) or similar tracking technologies that uniquely identify your browser to track your activity and hold certain information. Cookies can be necessary for site navigation and performance, or used for advertising. We use the following types of cookies:

- **Necessary Cookies** help you navigate on our websites and access certain features. For example, these cookies make sure your basket is saved during all the steps of your checkout process.
- **Functional Cookies** are there so we can give you an even smoother customer experience.
- **Performance Cookies** help us to understand the behavior of our visitors on our websites. This enables us to keep improving our Services.
- **Customer Interaction Cookies** are used for you to express your opinion. We love to know what you think about our Services. These cookies will enable you to like or recommend us on social media and to chat with our service agents. But you can also advise our other (potential) customers by rating and reviewing our products.
- **Advertising/Targeting Cookies** will remember your product and buying preferences. We believe advertising is all about relevance. These cookies enable us to share what you like with our advertisers. So if you are browsing the internet or using a social media app the advertisements you see can be more relevant to your preferences.

Our Services offer you the opportunity to decline “all but necessary” cookies. If you select that option when accessing the Services, we will not use any of the categories of cookies listed above, except for the Necessary Cookies.

Additionally, most web browsers accept cookies automatically but can be configured not to do so or to notify the user when a cookie is being sent. If you wish to disable cookies, refer to your browser help menu to learn how to disable cookies. Please note that if you disable all cookies, you may not be able to use some features of our websites.

Finally, we honor browser-based opt-out preference signals, including Global Privacy Control (“GPC”), where required by law. If we detect a valid GPC signal, we will treat it as a request to opt out of targeted advertising, sale, or sharing of personal information, as applicable.

**From third parties** . We may collect personal information about you from third parties, either at your request or for our business purposes. These third-party sources vary, but could include the following:

- **Technical Service Partners.** We work with a variety of third-party service providers to enhance the Services and make it function the way you expect. Our technical service partners provide website hosting, marketing, website usage analytics, advertising, and maintenance services. Many of these categories overlap with the passively collected personal information described above. In working with our service providers, we may share personal information with them and receive personal information back from them so that we may improve the Services.
- **Payment Processors.** We use third parties to process your payments. These third-party solutions are integrated into the Services so you may not always realize the information is not stored by us.
