# Who We Are

AN INNOVATIVE, PEOPLE-FIRST PLATFORM FOR CRITICAL POWER SERVICES

PowerChampions is building a best-in-class commercial critical power services platform—one that puts people first, delivers exceptional customer service, and creates real opportunity for the hardworking teams that keep this industry running. We are not just an investment firm seeking to deploy capital. Rather, we’re business-builders—entrepreneurs who understand what it takes to start, organically grow, and protect a company using levers such as training and technology.

#### Our Vision

To elevate the critical power industry through operational innovation, training, and a people-first culture.

![vision](https://www.power-champions.com/hs-fs/hubfs/vision.webp?width=500&name=vision.webp)

![PowerChampions-Mission](https://www.power-champions.com/hs-fs/hubfs/PowerChampions-Mission.jpg?width=936&height=800&name=PowerChampions-Mission.jpg)

## Our Mission

- **Empower Teams**: We foster a growth-oriented, collaborative culture with strong training (through our in-house *American Trades Academy*), career development, and generational wealth creation (through our team member ownership program)

- **Enhance Customer Trust**: We build lasting relationships through
transparency, technical excellence, and consistent reliability

- **Respect Legacies**: Our market-partners have put blood, sweat, and tears into their business. We commit to providing them with a fair, efficient transition that preserves their culture & upholds their commitment to excellence.

- **Fuel Growth**: We provide operational expertise, cutting-edge technology and training, and financial resources to unlock each company’s full potential

## The Values That Power Us

At PowerChampions, our strength comes from the character of our people. Every
individual in our network lives by four values:

#### Servant-Leadership

We lead by empowering others. True leadership begins with service — supporting our teams, customers, and communities so they can thrive. We believe success is shared and built on humility, accountability, and a genuine desire to see others win.

#### Empathy

Every project, partnership, and interaction begins with listening. We understand before we act. By putting ourselves in the shoes of others — customers, technicians, and teammates — we build trust, deliver better solutions, and create lasting relationships.

#### Integrity

We do what’s right, every time. Integrity is the foundation of Power Champions. Whether on a service call or in the boardroom, we hold ourselves to the highest ethical standards — doing what’s right, even when no one is watching.

#### Sincerity

We mean what we say and follow through with heart. We approach every commitment with authenticity and honesty. Our sincerity fuels strong partnerships and ensures that every promise we make is backed by genuine care and consistent action.

![PowerChampions_Who_We_Are](https://www.power-champions.com/hs-fs/hubfs/PowerChampions_Who_We_Are.jpg?width=936&height=800&name=PowerChampions_Who_We_Are.jpg)

## How We Support Growth across the platform

- **Operational Expertise**: We bring deep experience and provide hands-on support across all functions—recruiting, field operations, training, marketing, finance, technology, and compliance—so our teams can focus on delivering great service

- **Financial Strength**: Backed by experienced, values-aligned investors, we invest in our team, expanding its reach, and unlocking long-term potential—without compromising its hard-won culture and reputation

- **Talent and Training**: We prioritize people. That means best-in-class training (*American Trades Academy*), building a long-term talent pipeline, and team member ownership.

- **Technology and Insights**: From dispatch to data, we bring modern tools to boost efficiency, drive growth, and support better decision-making
