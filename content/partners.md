## Frequently Asked Questions About Selling Your Business

### How do I know when it's the right time to sell my business?

The right time depends on your financial goals, succession plans, growth opportunities, and personal priorities. Some owners explore selling when they're ready to retire, while others consider a partnership to support expansion or bring additional expertise into their business.

### What should I consider before selling my electrical business?

Before selling an electrical contracting business, consider its financial performance, customer relationships, employee retention, operational processes, and long-term growth opportunities. It's also important to evaluate whether a potential buyer shares your priorities for the company's future.

### Can I sell my generator service company to a strategic partner?

Generator service company owners may consider strategic partnerships as an alternative to operating independently or pursuing a traditional exit. The right arrangement depends on the business's circumstances, the owner's objectives, and the terms discussed with potential partners.

### How is the value of my business determined?

Business valuations often consider profitability, cash flow, customer concentration, recurring revenue, management structure, and growth potential. The specific valuation process and transaction structure will depend on the business and the potential buyer.

### What happens to my employees if I sell my company?

Employee continuity is an important consideration during a business sale. Before moving forward, discuss how a potential partner approaches employee retention, career development, leadership continuity, and company culture.

### How can I start a conversation about selling my business?

Start by identifying your goals and the type of future you envision for your company. From there, reach out to the PowerChampions team to explore whether a strategic partnership might align with your priorities.
