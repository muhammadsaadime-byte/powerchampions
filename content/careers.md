CAREERS AT POWERCHAMPIONS

# Your career in critical power starts here.

We keep communities, businesses, and critical facilities powered — by empowering the people who make it happen. Join a team where your talent, drive, and purpose move the mission forward.

[View open roles](https://www.power-champions.com/careers#open-roles)

## We Invest in People

Our success begins and ends with people. That’s why PowerChampions invests in every technician, engineer, and team member — providing the tools, training, and support they need to thrive. From hands-on fieldwork to leadership roles, we give you room to grow and the confidence to keep moving forward. We believe that when our people succeed, our partners and communities do too.

## We're Empowering People Every Step of the Way

Whether you’re maintaining systems in the field or leading a project team, every role at PowerChampions matters. Our culture is built on trust, training, and teamwork — helping you reach your full potential while powering what matters most.
